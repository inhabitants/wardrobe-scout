# Shop guide

Search addresses change over time. If one of these stops working, use the search box on the shop's home page.

## Vinted

Europe-wide second-hand marketplace (one site per country: vinted.pt, vinted.fr, vinted.es, vinted.it, vinted.de, vinted.nl, vinted.co.uk and others). The best place for everyday basics, workwear, shoes and cheap jewellery.

- **Search:** `https://www.vinted.<country>/catalog?search_text=<words>&order=relevance` (or `order=price_low_to_high`).
- **Cross-border:** in the EU many listings come from other countries and keep their original titles. Search in English, French, Spanish, Italian and the local language.
- **Result cards:** each card's link title carries the title, brand, condition, size and price. Reading those titles is enough to shortlist.
- **Listing page:** brand, size, condition, material, colour, upload date and the seller's description. A "Sold" banner means it is gone.
- **Price:** the shown price includes the buyer protection fee. Shipping appears as "Shipping from" only when signed in, and the cheapest option (usually a pick-up point) is the "from" value. Each seller ships separately.
- **Extras:** pricier items may offer an optional paid item verification at checkout. "Make an offer" exists; only the person uses it.

## Depop

Strong in the UK, the US and Australia; younger, streetwear and vintage-heavy.

- **Search:** `https://www.depop.com/search/?q=<words>`.
- **Sizes:** often US or UK. Convert for the person.
- **Price:** the listing shows the price and the shipping to the person's country; some sellers ship only domestically.
- **Watch for:** "vintage" used loosely, and reproductions sold as originals.

## Vestiaire Collective

Designer and luxury resale, with an authentication service for many items. The place for branded deals (designer jewellery, shoes, coats).

- **Search:** `https://www.vestiairecollective.com/search/?q=<words>`.
- **Price:** fees, authentication and shipping are added at checkout. Read the total before calling something a deal.
- **Watch for:** sizes in the brand's own system; ask for measurements.

## eBay

Global, with a site per country (ebay.com, ebay.co.uk, ebay.de, ebay.fr, ebay.it, ebay.es). Good for branded jewellery, military surplus, workwear and shoes.

- **Search:** `https://www.ebay.<country>/sch/i.html?_nkw=<words>`. Use the page filters for "Used" condition, "Buy it now" and the person's location.
- **Price:** check postage to the person's country and, outside the person's customs area, import charges.
- **Watch for:** auctions that end with a higher price than shown, and sellers far away with long delivery times.
