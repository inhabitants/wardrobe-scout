Steel signet ring

Stainless steel does not tarnish. Ring size is the inner diameter in millimetres.

  1. Wrap a thread around the finger where the ring will sit.
  2. Mark where it closes and measure the length in millimetres.
  3. Divide by 3.14. That is the diameter the listings show.
